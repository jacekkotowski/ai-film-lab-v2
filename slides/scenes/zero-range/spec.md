# zero-range — slide 5 of 5 of the zeroing film

**Problem (Jacek's script, section 3, second sentence):** the Aurora MIL
reticle's width marks range a target of known width. Metric only.

**One idea:** a 50 cm wide target fills a smaller mark the further away
it is, so the mark it fills tells the distance.

**Drawn:** the centre of the real reticle (`aimanim/aurora.py`, measured
and checked in `scenes/zero-reticle/spec.md`), 1.3 mil each side and 4.4
mil down, enlarged to 1.6 frame units per mil. To scale, widths and spacing.

**Width: a target 50 cm wide** (unchanged from 2026-10-05)
- the manual: chevron and 2nd/3rd/4th MIL stadia = 18 in at 300/400/500/600 yd.
  18 in = ½ yd and 50 cm = ½ m, so the same marks are **50 cm at
  300/400/500/600 m**: width in mil = 500 / metres
  = 1.667 / 1.250 / 1.000 / 0.833 mil
- check with the manual's formula, distance (m) = size (cm) × 10 / mils:
  50 × 10 / 1.667 = 300.0 m (and 400.0, 500.0, 600.0)

**Steps (one per sentence he says)**
0. before the first word: title "Ranging"
1. line 0 — the centre: chevron, bars 2–4, the dots beside them
2. line 1 — the 50 cm width over the chevron; "300 m"
3. line 2 — the same 50 cm over bars 2, 3, 4; "400 / 500 / 600 m"

**Narration (his script's sentence, made metric and split in three)**
"Its width marks also range targets. A target fifty centimetres wide
fills the central arrowhead at 300 metres, and the next three marks at
400, 500 and 600 metres."

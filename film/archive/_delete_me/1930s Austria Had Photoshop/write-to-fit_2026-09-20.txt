In 1930, Gottfried Spiegler and Kalman Juris faced a massive issue with X-ray plates. Medical radiographs had way too much dynamic range for photographic printing paper. Dense rib bones blocked radiation and appeared solid white, while lung tissue let radiation straight through, turning pitch black.

# face-ledger — slide 3 of love-as-a-face-threatening-act

**Problem (Jacek's words):** Declaring love is a face-threatening act. It
is a threat for all. The speaker puts his face on the table. The hearer
receives a weight. A question she did not ask for.

**One idea:** declaring love costs both people face, each a different
face: the speaker risks being accepted, the hearer loses some of being free.

**Data** (no numbers: face has no unit)
- Two faces per person, from the slide before (Brown and Levinson):
  "accepted" = positive face, the wish to be accepted; "free" = negative
  face, the wish to be free, unimposed on. His words, P2.
- The ledger: one bar per face per person, all the same length (3.0 units)
  at the start. The lengths are a picture, not a measurement.
- The weight takes 0.4 of her "free" bar (1.2 of 3.0). Chosen so it reads
  on a phone, not measured; Brown and Levinson's weight (W = D + P + R)
  has no scale.
- Speaker ACCENT, hearer SECOND, the weight SICK.

**Steps (one per sentence he says)**
0. before the first word: title "Declaring love"; speaker and hearer with
   a table between them; the ledger, rows "accepted" and "free", four full bars
1. line 0, "Declaring" — "I love you" written between them
2. line 2, "puts" — the speaker's "accepted" bar moves onto the table; its
   place in the ledger stays as an outline
3. line 3, "receives" — a weight drops onto the hearer's "free" bar and
   takes 0.4 of it
4. line 4, "question" — "?" on the weight

Line 1 ("It is a threat for all.") has no step: the still shows it.

# face-weight — slide 2 of love-as-a-face-threatening-act

**Problem (Jacek's words):** Brown and Levinson split face again. Positive
face: the wish to be accepted. Negative face: the wish to be free,
unimposed on. Any act that touches these is a face-threatening act. Its
weight is distance plus power plus the size of the imposition.

**One idea:** a person has two faces to threaten, "accepted" and "free";
a threat's weight is W = D + P + R.

**Data** (no numbers: nothing here is measured)
- Two bars, as in the ledger of slides 03-05 (SECOND, 6.0 long here, one
  person). The red weight takes the outer WEIGHT_SHARE (0.4) of "free"
  with "W" in it, as the "?" does in face-ledger.
- "W = D + P + R" (Brown and Levinson's W_x = D(S,H) + P(H,S) + R_x),
  then a legend: D distance, P power, R imposition. R is their "ranking of
  imposition"; he says "the size of the imposition".
- Session 1's plan had three blocks D, P, R stacking into the weight;
  this one has the formula and a legend instead (fewer moving parts).
  Never agreed by Jacek, either way.
- Title "The weight" (Claude's words; not narrated).

**Steps (one per sentence he says)**
1. line 1, "accepted" — "accepted" and its bar
2. line 2, "free" — "free" and its bar
3. line 3, "touches" — the red weight lands on "free", and the formula appears
4. line 4, "distance" — D distance
5. line 4, "power" — P power
6. line 4, "size" — R imposition

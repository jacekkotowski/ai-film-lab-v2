### Why I love you

Why do I love you?

Because I want to see you live.

I want to witness the awakening you are searching for.

I want to see what happens

when you finally become fully yourself.

I want you to be free.

Truly free.

Free to create.

Free to grow.

Free to give life.

Free to do good.

And if you are afraid,

I want to walk with you.

Not to choose your path for you.

Just to walk beside you.

And if you fall,

I want to be there to catch you.

Then help you stand again.

Because I believe there is something in you

that is worth protecting.

Something that wants to wake up.

Something that wants to live.

So when fear comes,

remember what God keeps saying:

**Do not be afraid.**

You do not have to know

the whole road.

You only have to take

the next step.

And I want to see you take it.

I want to see you succeed.

Not for me.

For you.

Because I love you.

And perhaps this is what love means to me:

to want another person

to become more alive,

more free,

more capable of giving goodness to the world.

I do not want to possess your freedom.

I want to see what you will do with it.

And if I am allowed

to walk beside you for some of the road,

I will.

With no claim on you.

Only love.

# face-fail — slide 7 of love-as-a-face-threatening-act

**Problem (Jacek's words):** By Grice's own measure, they fail. A slur
breaks the maxim of quality: it says what is false. An old picture breaks
the maxim of relation: it has nothing to do with who she is now. They aim
at mianzi, her reputation. They cannot reach lian. Lian is the one that
chooses goodness. Here and now.

**One idea:** the insults break two of Grice's maxims, and they hit
mianzi but stop at lian.

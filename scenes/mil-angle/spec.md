**Problem (Jacek's plan, 1:05–1:25):** 1 MIL @ 100 m = 10 cm, @ 200 m =

**One idea:** a mil is an angle, so the length it covers grows with distance.

**Data**
- 1 mil = 1/1000 rad; covered length = distance × tan(0.001) ≈ distance / 1000:

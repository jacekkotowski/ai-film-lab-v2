# face-two — slide 1 of love-as-a-face-threatening-act

**Problem (Jacek's words):** Every time we speak, we risk face. Erving
Goffman called face the positive value a person claims for herself in
front of others. Chinese thought splits it in two. Hu Hsien Chin described
both in 1944: mianzi, the face of prestige and reputation, and lian, the
face of moral character. You can lose mianzi and keep lian. Lian is what
you are when nobody claps.

**One idea:** face is two rings: mianzi outside, lit by the crowd; lian
inside, which stays when the crowd is gone.

**Data** (no numbers: nothing here is measured)
- Rings from aimanim/faces.py (shared with face-fail): centre (0, 2),
  mianzi r 3.4 (SECOND), lian r 1.8; the one face before the split r 2.6.
- The crowd: kit.people, 11 + 10, pitch 0.7, 0.6 tall, rows at y −2.3, −3.2.
- Title "Two faces" (Claude's words; not narrated).

**Steps (one per sentence he says)**
1. line 0, "face" — one ring, "face" in it
2. line 2, "splits" — it becomes two rings, "face" goes
3. line 3, "mianzi" — "mianzi" in the outer band, the crowd below
4. line 3, "lian" — "lian" in the inner ring
5. line 4, "lose" — the crowd goes, mianzi dims
6. line 5, "what" (Lian is what you are) — lian turns gold

I bought a medallion myself once. Cheap silver, stamped. Mother and child. I carried it close to my body, for years.

Feminism today says something else. Justyna Melonowska writes it plainly: its fulfillment is the murder of the child... the recognition of that murder as the "law of laws." From care to hatred. From mother to childless. She calls it the inheritance of Eve.

Melonowska writes: "Feminism today is counter-wisdom. The more of it, the more power. The more power, the less wisdom."

I bought a cheap medallion instead. Mother and child. Not power as the cosmic principle of femininity. Just someone holding someone.

She writes: "Thought is Mother. Hence philosophy is maternal."

Cheap silver. Stamped. That's what I carry.

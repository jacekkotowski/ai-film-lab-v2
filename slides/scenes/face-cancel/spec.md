# face-cancel — slide 5 of love-as-a-face-threatening-act

**Problem (Jacek's words):** Grice also gave us the way out. Implicatures
can be cancelled. I love you. You owe me nothing. No answer. No return. The
love stays. The demand is gone. The speaker pays the whole cost. The hearer
carries nothing.

**One idea:** cancelling the implicature moves the weight: it leaves the
hearer's "free" and the speaker carries it; "I love you" stays.

**Data** (no numbers measured: face has no unit)
- Opens on face-ledger's last picture, from aimanim/ledger.py: speaker and
  hearer at x ±2.4, "I love you" on baseline 4.9, his "accepted" bar on
  the table with its empty slot, her "free" bar cut to 1.8 of 3.0 under the
  red weight (0.4 × 3.0 = 1.2 wide, 1.1 tall) with its "?".
- After: her "free" back to 3.0; his "free" 1.8 under the same weight (his
  outer 1.2, x −3.9 to −2.7). Nothing else changes.
- Title "The way out" (his sentence 0).

**Steps (one per sentence he says)**
0. before the first word: face-ledger's end, title "The way out"
1. line 1, "cancelled" — a white strike through the "?"
2. line 7, "gone" — the struck weight lifts off her bar to the middle
   (0, −2.8), between the two "free" bars; her bar full again
3. line 8, "pays" — the weight lands on his "free" bar, his bar cut to 1.8

Lines 2-6 ("I love you. You owe me nothing. No answer. No return. The love
stays.") and 9 ("The hearer carries nothing.") are said over the picture.

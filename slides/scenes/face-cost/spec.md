# face-cost — slide 10 of love-as-a-face-threatening-act

**Problem (Jacek's words):** The insult is cheap. The speaker risks
nothing and takes face from someone else. The declaration is expensive.
The speaker risks everything and gives face away.

**One idea:** the insult moves face from her to him; the declaration
moves face from him to her.

**Data** (no numbers: the lengths are a picture)
- Two small ledgers, one above the other: "insult" (SICK) and
  "declaration" (ACCENT), each with speaker / hearer and one face bar
  each. Bars as in aimanim/ledger.py (outwards from BAR_IN 0.9) but
  2.0 long, so a moved piece (0.9) still ends inside the frame (3.85).
- "insult": the hearer's outer piece goes to the end of the speaker's
  bar, and her empty slot stays outlined. "declaration": the speaker's
  piece goes to the hearer, and his slot stays outlined.
- Under each ledger, its price: "cheap", "expensive" (his words).
- Title "Who pays" (Claude's words; not narrated). "Cheap and
  expensive" is 11.85 wide at 80, too wide even at 56.

**Steps (one per sentence he says)**
1. line 0, "insult" — the insult's ledger, "cheap"
2. line 1, "takes" — her piece moves to him
3. line 2, "declaration" — the declaration's ledger, "expensive"
4. line 3, "everything" — his piece moves to her

A Prayer for Her

Let the morning find her gently,
not with alarms, but with light
laid softly on the pillow
like a hand that asks for nothing.

Let the day be kind to her hands.
They carry more than anyone sees:
the cups, the worries, the small repairs
that hold a life together.

When she is tired, let her rest
without apologizing for it.
When she is doubted, let her remember
the voice inside that never lied to her.

Give her one true friend at every door,
bread that tastes like someone cared,
and a laugh that surprises her,
the kind that comes up from the ground.

Where she has been wounded,
do not erase the scar.
Let it shine a little,
the way old silver does, where it was held most.

And when the evening closes,
let her know, without being told,
that she was seen today,
entirely, and loved exactly so.

# scr-meaning — slide 3 of screening

**Problem (Jacek's words):** Of 517 flagged women, 18 carry an affected
baby, PPV 1 in 29. Of 9,483 cleared, 9,481 are healthy, NPV 99.98 %.
Gigerenzer's 160 gynecologists most often answered 9 in 10, not 1 in 10.

**One idea:** read the table by rows: a positive here means 1 in 29.

**Data**
- flagged row: 18 + 499 = 517; PPV = 18 / 517 = 3.48 % = 1 in 28.7 → "1 in 29"
- cleared row: 2 + 9,481 = 9,483; NPV = 9,481 / 9,483 = 99.979 % → "99.98%"
- each row drawn with ONE dot size (517 at r 0.055, 9,483 at r 0.014),
  affected ones red and scattered (seeds 1 and 2); the 2 in the cleared
  row ringed, else they cannot be found
- Gigerenzer (mammography): most frequent answer 90 % (47 % of 160); the
  true PPV there about 10 %. Animation list: "9 in 10" crossed out, "1 in 10"

**Steps**
0. title "A positive result"
1. line 0, "Of" — "PPV: 517 flagged", the 517 dots, "18 / 517 = 1 in 29"
2. line 1, "cleared" — "NPV: 9,483 cleared", the 9,483 dots + 2 rings, "99.98% healthy"
3. line 2, "Gerd" — "9 in 10" struck (doctors) | "1 in 10" (truth)

Layout note "9 in 10 touches a Line": the strike-through, meant.
"9,481 / 9,483 = 99.98%" is 8.9 units at 56, wider than the slide: shown
as "99.98% healthy" under the header with 9,483.

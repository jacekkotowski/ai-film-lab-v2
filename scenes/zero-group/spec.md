# zero-group — slide 1 of 4 of the zeroing film

**Problem (Jacek's script, section 1 "Find the centre of the group"):**
four shots at one aim point; where does the rifle really shoot, and how
far is that from the aim?

**One idea:** the middle of the group, not any single hole, is where the
rifle shoots; its offset from the aim point is what you correct.

**Data** (at 100 m; cm from the aim point, + = right / up)
- holes: (−6, −3.5), (−3, −4), (−5.5, −6.5), (−3.5, −6)
- centre = mean of the four = (−4.5, −5.0) → **5 cm low, 4.5 cm left**
- largest distance of a hole from the centre: 2.1 cm
- hole drawn at 5.56 mm (radius 0.28 cm), to scale with the grid

**Steps (one per sentence he says)**
0. before the first word, already on screen: title, 1 cm grid, aim point, "100 m"
1. line 0 — the four holes appear
2. line 1 — the centre is marked; the holes dim
3. line 2 — the offset: 4.5 cm left, then 5 cm down to the centre
(line 3, "if one hole strays…", has no step: nothing new to draw)

**Narration (his own words, from the script)**
"Fire four shots at the same point from a sandbag or bipod. Mark the
middle of the four holes, because that middle is where the rifle really
shoots while single shots scatter. Then measure how far it sits from
your aim point. If one hole strays far from the rest, fire a fresh group."

**Downstream (slide 2, same numbers):** 1 MOA at 100 m = 2.909 cm.
Triumph ½ MOA = 1.454 cm/click: 5 / 1.454 = 3.44 → 3 up, 4.5 / 1.454 =
3.09 → 3 right. SLx ¼ MOA = 0.727 cm/click: 6.88 → 7 up, 6.19 → **6**
right (the script's "seven and five" was for 4 cm; now "seven and six").

**Sources checked 2026-10-05** (for the whole film)
- Vortex Triumph manual M-00433-0: ½ MOA per click, ~0.50 in at 100 yd
- Primary Arms SLx 5x MicroPrism, Aurora MIL product page and both manuals: 0.25 MOA click
- newer "5x MicroPrism Optic Manual": UP = elevation counterclockwise,
  RIGHT = windage counterclockwise (older manual says the opposite);
  Jacek's own elevation cap: counterclockwise = up (windage cap: not yet checked)
- ACSS Aurora MIL manual: chevron and 2nd/3rd/4th MIL stadia = 18 in at 300/400/500/600 yd

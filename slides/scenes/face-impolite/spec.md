# face-impolite — slide 6 of love-as-a-face-threatening-act

**Problem (Jacek's words, two paragraphs):** Now look at the other speech
acts aimed at her. The crowds pass around her old pictures. They call her
names I will not repeat. Jonathan Culpeper studied this and called it
impoliteness: speech built to attack face on purpose. Bald, on record, no
redress. Brown and Levinson mapped how people soften threats. These people
do the opposite. They maximise the damage.

**One idea:** politeness climbs away from the bare threat ("bald, on
record"); impoliteness goes the other way, below it.

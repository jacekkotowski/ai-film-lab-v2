# back-azimuth — the trial scene

**Problem (Jacek's words, paraphrased):** a scout walks from A to B on a
bearing; how do they get back? The back-azimuth in mils.

**One idea:** back = forward ± 3200 mil (half of 6400).

**Data**
- forward azimuth A→B: 1200 mil
- full circle: 6400 mil
- back azimuth B→A: 1200 + 3200 = 4400 mil

**Steps (one per sentence he will say)**
1. the mil circle, north at the top, 0 / 1600 / 3200 / 4800
2. the walk A→B, arrow at 1200 mil
3. at B, north again; the way back, arrow at 4400 mil
4. the sum: 1200 + 3200 = 4400 mil

**Suggested narration (≈ 30 words, he says it his own way)**
"A full circle is 6400 mils. From A we walk to B on twelve hundred.
To come back, add half the circle. Twelve hundred plus thirty-two
hundred: forty-four hundred."

# scr-cost — slide 5 of screening

**Problem (Jacek's words):** Each of the 499 false alarms is a mother told
her baby may be ill. She can wait until week 15 for amniocentesis, then up
to two weeks for the result. That needle ends about 3 pregnancies in 1,000,
so if all 499 take it, one or two healthy babies are lost.

**One idea:** the false positives have a price: weeks of fear, and about
1.5 healthy pregnancies lost per 10,000 screened.

**Data**
- 499 false positives (slide 2)
- weeks: flagged 12, amniocentesis 15 (offered 15–20), result ~17 (10 days–2 weeks)
- procedure-related loss after amniocentesis 0.30 % (95 % CI 0.11–0.49 %)
- 499 × 0.003 = 1.497 → "≈ 1.5"; an upper end (many now take the blood test first)
- 1.5 shown as one figure orange, one half orange (two fading figures in
  499 cannot be seen on a phone; checked on the full-size still)

**Steps**
0. title "499 false alarms"; the 499 as dots
1. line 0, "Each" — the dots become people (kit.people)
2. line 1, "wait" — timeline: week 12 flagged, week 15 needle
3. line 1, "result" — week 17 result
4. line 2, "needle" — two figures orange (1, ½); "499 × 0.3% ≈ 1.5 / healthy
   pregnancies / lost per 10,000"

"per 10,000 screened" is 8.09 units at 56, past the safe width: the slide
says "lost per 10,000"; the narration says the rest.

**Sources:** Salomon LJ et al. 2019 (UOG): amniocentesis 0.30 %; timing from
his bibliography. 1.5 calculated.
